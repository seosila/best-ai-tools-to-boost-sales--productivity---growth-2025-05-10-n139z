How We Choose Tools

SellBetterAI is a growing directory. When considering a tool for a listing, we look at:

  • Relevance to the directory and the category.
  • The tool's intended use and the audience it is meant to serve.
  • Whether the available product information is clear enough to describe the tool accurately.
  • Whether an official website is available to help visitors find current information.
  • The usefulness and source of the evidence available to support the listing details.

A directory listing summarizes available information; it is not, by itself, an independent hands-on review or a claim that we have personally tested the product. The amount of information and evidence can vary by listing.

Paid placements and sponsorships

SellBetterAI may offer paid featured listings and sponsored articles. Paid featured placements will be clearly labeled so readers can distinguish commercial visibility from ordinary directory listings. Payment may provide additional visibility through a designated featured placement, but it does not determine our editorial conclusions or independently establish a tool's quality, suitability, or performance. Sponsored articles will be identified as sponsored content.

No paid listings or sponsored articles have been published on SellBetterAI as of the date of this policy. This statement will be updated when that changes.

Product features and pricing may change. Confirm current details, terms, and prices on the vendor's official website before making a decision.
