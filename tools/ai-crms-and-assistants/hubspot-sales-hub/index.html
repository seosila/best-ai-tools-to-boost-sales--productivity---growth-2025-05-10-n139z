HubSpot Sales Hub

HubSpot Sales Hub brings customer records and sales workflows into HubSpot's customer platform. Its CRM, pipeline, engagement, and reporting features can be considered alongside AI-assisted prospecting options, plan limits, and the team's expected use.

CRM and pipeline capabilities

Sales Hub documents CRM records, deal pipeline management, meeting scheduling, sales engagement, and reporting. These capabilities are intended to support the organization of customer information and sales activity within HubSpot's customer platform.

AI-assisted sales features

HubSpot documents AI-assisted sales features including Prospecting Agent and Data Agent. Their availability and applicable usage allowances depend on the plan and current product terms; do not assume every AI capability is included in the free tier.

Who may find Sales Hub useful

Teams already organizing customer work in HubSpot, or comparing CRM-based approaches to pipeline and engagement management, may want to assess Sales Hub. Consider how the team's records, sales stages, meeting process, reporting needs, and any AI-assisted tasks would fit together.

Free plan, paid tiers, and onboarding

HubSpot offers free tools, and its official pricing page lists a free tier for up to two users. Paid plans have different seat pricing, feature limits, and credit allowances. Professional and Enterprise plans can require one-time onboarding fees; the current official page lists $1,500 for Professional onboarding and $3,500 for Enterprise onboarding. These are onboarding fees, not monthly subscription prices.

Pricing can vary by billing period, seat count, and credits. Compare the plan configuration and required onboarding costs on HubSpot's official pricing page.

How to compare plans

  • Count the seats needed and check how each plan defines seat pricing.
  • Identify the features, AI tools, and credit allowances needed for the workflow.
  • Include applicable onboarding charges and billing terms when estimating total cost.
Features, availability, and pricing may change. Confirm current details directly with HubSpot before making a decision.
