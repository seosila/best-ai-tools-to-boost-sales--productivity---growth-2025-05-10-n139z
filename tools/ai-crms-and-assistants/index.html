Choose around team size, workflow, and total cost

Begin with the number of users and the customer and pipeline workflows the team needs to manage. Compare how each platform fits existing processes, how much configuration or implementation the team expects to take on, and which AI features are actually included in the relevant plan. Estimate total cost using seats, billing terms, usage or credits, required onboarding, and any add-ons—not just a headline plan price.

Features, availability, and pricing may change. Confirm plan details, eligibility, and costs directly with the vendor.
