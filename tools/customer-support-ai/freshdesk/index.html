Customer support helpdesk and AI assistance

Freshdesk

Freshdesk is Freshworks' customer support helpdesk for organizing service requests, collaborating through a shared inbox, and providing self-service resources. Its Freddy AI capabilities include a customer-facing AI Agent as well as tools that assist human agents and support analysis.

Ticketing and service workflows

Freshdesk documents ticketing, shared inbox collaboration, threads and tasks, a customer portal and knowledge base, analytics, and routing or service-level features. Channel availability and features depend on the Freshdesk plan and product configuration; Freshdesk and Freshdesk Omni should not be treated as the same package when checking channel coverage.

Freddy AI Agent, Copilot, and Insights

Freddy AI Agent is customer-facing and can respond to customers using help content and supported workflows. Freddy AI Copilot is for human agents, with functions Freshworks describes such as drafting replies, summarizing conversations, translation, and surfacing relevant context. Freddy AI Insights provides support analytics and is plan-dependent. These are distinct capabilities; an AI agent that responds to customers is not the same as an assistant that supports a human responder.

Pricing and AI usage

Freshdesk's pricing page currently lists Growth, Pro, and Enterprise plans billed per agent, with annual-billing prices displayed separately from monthly billing. The page currently includes the first 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions on each of those plans; additional sessions are listed at $49 per 100. Freddy AI Copilot is a paid add-on listed at $29 per agent per month when billed annually, available on Pro and Enterprise. Freshdesk also lists other usage-based charges for some add-ons and actions. Confirm current plan eligibility, billing cadence, and usage terms before budgeting.

Strengths and limitations

  • Provides conventional ticketing, shared inbox, knowledge base, and customer-portal workflows alongside optional AI capabilities.
  • The separation between customer-facing AI, agent assistance, and insights gives teams distinct capabilities to evaluate against their support process.
  • AI features are not all included in the same way: session limits, eligible plans, add-ons, and additional usage charges affect total cost.
  • Teams should map the required channels to the exact Freshdesk product and plan; some omnichannel capabilities are described under Freshdesk Omni rather than the base Freshdesk plans.

Who should consider Freshdesk?

Teams that need a structured helpdesk for ticket assignment, collaboration, and self-service—and may want to add customer-facing AI or agent assistance—can assess Freshdesk by plan and expected usage. Organizations prioritizing an AI agent that can also be used with a current helpdesk may compare Intercom Fin; smaller teams seeking web chat and optional Lyro automation can review Tidio. Freshdesk may not be necessary for teams that only need a simple chat widget or lack the staffing and process to operate a ticket queue.

Plan prices, billing cadence, AI-session allowances, add-ons, and channel availability may change. Verify the current Freshdesk and Freshdesk Omni terms that apply to your support requirements.
