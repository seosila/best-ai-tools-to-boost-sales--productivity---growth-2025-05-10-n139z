Intercom Fin

AI customer service agent

Intercom Fin

Fin is Intercom's customer-facing AI agent for answering support questions and carrying out supported procedures. It can work within Intercom's service platform or alongside certain existing helpdesks, where it can pass unresolved conversations to a human team with context.

Visit Intercom Fin View official pricing

Customer-facing answers and support actions

Intercom describes Fin as working across channels including chat, email, voice, Slack, and social. It can use help content and connected business systems to answer questions or perform configured actions through supported integrations, APIs, and workflows. When a human needs to step in, Fin can hand off to the team's inbox while retaining conversation context.

Using Fin with an existing helpdesk

Fin is native to Intercom but Intercom also documents use with external helpdesks, including Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, and Freshdesk. Intercom's public pricing page describes a Fin-only setup for an existing helpdesk with no seats required, subject to a minimum monthly outcome commitment. Integration coverage, setup, handoff destinations, and any minimums should be confirmed against the helpdesk and current commercial terms before treating this as a drop-in replacement or add-on.

Outcome pricing versus platform and seat costs

Intercom currently advertises Fin from $0.99 per outcome. Its pricing explanation counts an outcome when the customer confirms resolution, does not ask for more help after Fin responds, or Fin completes a Procedure; it says one outcome fee applies per conversation even if multiple questions are answered. A completed Procedure may count even when it includes a handoff, so review the outcome definition carefully.

When using the complete Intercom platform, seat costs are separate from Fin usage and depend on plan and billing. When using Fin with another helpdesk, Intercom says seats are not required but a minimum monthly outcome commitment can apply (the public page gives 50 outcomes as an example). Public rates and minimums may not apply to every contract or volume level; confirm the current quote and whether any additional channel usage applies.

Strengths and trade-offs

  • It is focused on automating customer-facing resolution and supported actions rather than only suggesting text to human agents.
  • Existing-helpdesk integrations may let teams evaluate AI automation without migrating their full support operation, subject to integration capability and terms.
  • Outcome-based charges can be easier to relate to AI-resolved work, but teams need to understand which outcomes are chargeable and forecast usage alongside platform or channel costs.
  • It may be more capability than needed for teams seeking only a basic chat widget or ticket queue.

Who should consider Intercom Fin?

Support organizations looking to automate customer conversations across multiple channels, take actions in connected systems, or add AI resolution to a supported existing helpdesk may want to evaluate Fin. Teams that prioritize conventional ticketing and self-service can compare Freshdesk; smaller businesses seeking a combined live-chat and ticketing entry point can also review Tidio. Fin may not suit a team whose anticipated outcome volume or contract minimums make the usage model a poor fit.

Fin rates, outcome definitions, supported channels, and helpdesk terms can change. Confirm current pricing and integration details directly with Intercom.
