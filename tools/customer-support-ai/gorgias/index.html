Gorgias

Gorgias offers a customer-support helpdesk for ecommerce businesses, along with an AI Agent for customer conversations. Teams can evaluate its support workflows and usage-based costs against their ticket volume and service needs.

What Gorgias does

Gorgias provides customer-support software oriented toward ecommerce. Its helpdesk brings customer conversations into support workflows, while the AI Agent can assist with pre-purchase questions and post-purchase support. The vendor's documentation describes configuring its knowledge and actions and handing conversations to a human team when needed.

Who may find it useful

Ecommerce support teams evaluating a shared customer-service workspace and AI-assisted responses may consider Gorgias. Check channel requirements, integrations, escalation procedures, and the way tickets and AI resolutions are counted before choosing a plan.

Usage-based pricing

Gorgias pricing depends on ticket volume and plan terms. AI Agent resolutions may incur additional usage-based charges on top of helpdesk pricing, so total costs depend on support usage and resolved conversations. Check the live pricing page for current tiers, overages, and AI charges before estimating a budget.

Gorgias has an official affiliate program page. Review its current terms directly for participation and payout details; SellBetterAI is not affiliated with Gorgias.

Points to check before choosing Gorgias

  • Estimate monthly ticket volume and check how plan allowances and overages apply.
  • Understand how an AI Agent resolution is counted and what usage-based charges may apply.
  • Review the AI Agent's supported workflows, escalation behavior, and configuration requirements.
Features and usage charges may change. Confirm current details directly with Gorgias before making a decision.
