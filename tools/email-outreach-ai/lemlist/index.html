Email and multichannel sales engagement

lemlist

lemlist is a sales engagement platform for teams that want to coordinate outbound email with additional touchpoints. Its current plan lineup separates email-focused outreach from a multichannel plan that includes LinkedIn automation and calling features. Lead-data and enrichment options are also presented separately from engagement plans.

Last verified: 15 October 2026 · Product details reviewed against official vendor pages; not hands-on tested.

Visit official websiteView official pricing

What lemlist does

The platform combines outbound engagement workflows with personalization and deliverability features. Depending on the plan, teams can coordinate email with LinkedIn activity and calls, and use optional lead-data features to find or enrich prospect information. Check the current plan comparison to confirm which channels and allowances are included in your chosen subscription.

Who may find it useful

lemlist is worth evaluating when a sales team wants more than a sequence of emails—for example, a planned mix of email, LinkedIn touches, and calls with shared campaign context. It may be more than you need if you only want a focused cold-email sending tool and already have separate systems for prospect data and other channels.

Plans and billing

lemlist lists separate Email, Multichannel, and Enterprise engagement plans, with data-related add-ons available separately. The pricing page may vary by billing cycle, currency, volume, and promotion. Compare the actual plan configuration, per-user charges, sending capacity, data credits, and any add-ons before calculating total cost.

Strengths and points to check

  • Useful to compare when coordinating several outreach channels is part of the sales process.
  • Personalization and deliverability features still require sound list quality, sensible sending practices, and human review of messaging.
  • Verify channel availability, connected-account limits, data credits, integrations, and seat costs for the specific plan.
  • Follow the privacy, consent, and direct-marketing rules that apply in the countries where you contact prospects.

Compare it with other outreach tools

For email-first campaign execution, compare Instantly and Smartlead. If your priority is a broader sequence involving LinkedIn and calls, compare the channel support and pricing model directly with lemlist. For help improving individual email drafts, see Lavender, which addresses email coaching rather than campaign execution. For a broader comparison of outreach platforms, read the cold email outreach tools buying guide.

Features, plan limits, and pricing may change. Confirm current details directly with lemlist before making a decision.
