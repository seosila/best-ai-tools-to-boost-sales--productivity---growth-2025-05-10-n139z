Instantly

Instantly offers sales email outreach products and separate credit-based products for related prospecting workflows. Teams can review the available products and current terms to decide whether they fit their outbound process.

Last verified: 13 October 2026

Visit official website View official pricing

What Instantly does

Instantly's official materials describe email outreach and prospecting products for sales teams. The company presents outreach and credit products separately, so buyers should check which product is needed for the intended workflow.

Who may find it useful

It may be relevant to teams running outbound email campaigns or evaluating prospecting workflows that use a separate credit allowance. Before adopting it, consider the team's sending process, contact-data needs, and how usage is divided between the available products.

Plans and billing

At the time checked, Instantly's pricing page displayed a Growth plan at $47 per month for Email Outreach and a separate Growth Credits plan at $47 per month with 1,500 credits per month. These are separate products, not one combined charge. Pricing pages can show promotional offers and billing-period options; confirm the current selection and total on the official pricing page before purchasing.

Instantly's official affiliate page advertises 20–35% recurring commission, subject to program terms and application review. Approval is not guaranteed, and this directory is not affiliated with Instantly.

Points to check before choosing Instantly

  • Confirm whether Email Outreach, Credits, or a listed bundle covers the workflow you need.
  • Check the credit allowance, billing period, and any promotional pricing directly with Instantly.
  • Review sending requirements and applicable email laws for the markets where you operate.
Features, availability, and pricing may change. Confirm current details directly with Instantly before making a decision.
