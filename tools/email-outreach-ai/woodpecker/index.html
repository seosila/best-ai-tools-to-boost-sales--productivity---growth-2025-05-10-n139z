Cold email and outbound workflow management

Woodpecker

Woodpecker is an outbound platform for teams that need to build and manage cold-email campaigns, follow-ups, sender accounts, and replies. Its official feature list includes email verification, warm-up, inbox rotation, adaptive sending, domain checks, and integrations; LinkedIn outreach and agency functions are available as plan options or add-ons, so confirm the current setup before comparing costs.

Last verified: 15 October 2026 · Product details reviewed against official vendor pages; not hands-on tested.

Visit official websiteView official pricing

What Woodpecker does

The platform focuses on outbound execution: create email sequences, schedule follow-ups, organize sending accounts, review replies, and use verification and sending controls to reduce avoidable campaign problems. The official pricing page also lists optional features for LinkedIn outreach, agency management, and integrations. These features do not guarantee inbox placement or campaign performance.

Who may find it useful

Woodpecker is worth comparing for small outbound teams and agencies that want campaign automation and reply handling, with optional agency or multichannel workflows as their needs grow. Buyers should check how the contacted-prospect model, email volume, warm-up allowances, and add-ons map to their expected use.

Plans and billing

Woodpecker's pricing page uses contacted-prospect limits and presents different allowances for emails, stored prospects, warm-ups, and additional services. Optional charges may apply to connected LinkedIn accounts, agency clients, infrastructure, and some integrations. Prices and promotional offers can change, so compare the live plan configuration rather than relying on a single headline price.

Strengths and points to check

  • Useful to evaluate when verification, warm-up, campaign follow-ups, and reply management need to sit in one workflow.
  • Check how the contacted-prospect count is calculated, especially when prospects appear in multiple campaigns.
  • Confirm the price of required integrations, LinkedIn connections, agency features, and sending infrastructure.
  • Use permission-based, relevant targeting and follow the privacy and marketing laws applicable to your audience. Deliverability tools cannot replace list hygiene and responsible sending.

Compare it with other outreach tools

Compare Woodpecker with Instantly and Smartlead for email-first campaign management, and with lemlist if you need more explicit multichannel sales engagement. For a side-by-side buyer framework, read our cold email outreach tools buying guide.
