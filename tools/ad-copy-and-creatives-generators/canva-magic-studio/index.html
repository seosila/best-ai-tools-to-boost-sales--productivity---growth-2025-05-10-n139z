AI-assisted visual design in Canva

Canva Magic Studio

Magic Studio is Canva's collection of AI-assisted design and media features, not a separate design vendor or independent product subscription. It can help marketers create or adapt visual assets inside Canva. Unlike a dedicated ad-creative generator such as AdCreative.ai, Canva also serves broader design, editing, and presentation needs.

Explore Canva Magic Studio View Canva plans

What Magic Studio can help create

Canva's Magic Studio announcement describes features including Magic Design for generating designs from prompts, Magic Switch for transforming or translating designs, Magic Media for generating images and video, and editing tools such as Magic Grab, Magic Expand, and Magic Edit. Feature availability and usage limits can vary by Canva plan and feature; check the current product and plan details for the exact access available to your account.

Pricing and access

Magic Studio is part of Canva's product rather than a separate standalone vendor subscription. Canva's available plans and feature-level usage limits may differ, so review its current official plan information and the details shown for each AI feature. No separate Magic Studio price is listed here.

Strengths and considerations

  • Having design, editing, and AI-assisted adaptation in one Canva workspace may suit marketers who create multiple kinds of visual assets.
  • Magic Studio is not a dedicated ad campaign management or performance-optimization system; teams needing ad-specific production should compare tools such as AdCreative.ai.
  • Access and usage allowances vary across features and plans, so verify the exact capability and limits before building a workflow around them.
  • Canva cautions that AI-generated outputs can be inaccurate; review factual content, visual details, brand fit, and usage rights before publishing.

Who should consider Canva Magic Studio?

Marketing and small-business teams already using Canva, or teams that need to produce and adapt a variety of visual materials, may want to evaluate Magic Studio. It may be less relevant for buyers seeking only specialized ad generation or social scheduling. For social post creation and publishing, see Predis.ai.
