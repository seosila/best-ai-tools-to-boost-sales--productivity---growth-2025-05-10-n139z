ChatGPT

General-purpose AI assistant

ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational assistant for working through questions and tasks in natural language. It may help with drafting and rewriting, summarizing material, explaining topics, brainstorming, and learning. OpenAI describes additional capabilities such as working with supported files and using tools; exact availability depends on the current product, account, settings, and usage limits.

Visit ChatGPT

Best suited to interactive knowledge work

ChatGPT is a reasonable fit when a person can describe a task, provide context, iterate on the response, and review the result. Common evaluation scenarios include producing a first draft, summarizing a document, exploring ideas, explaining unfamiliar material, or getting help with a multistep work task. Treat the output as assistance for a human-led workflow, not as a substitute for subject-matter judgment.

Not the same as a customer-support chatbot platform

The ChatGPT product is a general-purpose assistant that people interact with; it should not be assumed to be a ready-to-deploy website support bot connected to a company's help center, ticket queue, or escalation process. OpenAI's API is a separate developer platform for building applications, which requires implementation work. Teams seeking managed customer-facing support workflows can compare Intercom Fin, Gorgias, and Tidio; verify each product's actual integrations, configuration, handoff, and plan details.

Limitations and responsible use

  • ChatGPT can produce incorrect or misleading answers. Check important facts and sources independently, especially before acting on sensitive or high-impact information.
  • Responses depend on the prompt and the information available in the conversation or enabled tools. Ask focused questions and provide relevant context, but do not treat a confident answer as proof.
  • Features and usage limits can vary by plan, account, settings, and availability. Confirm current access and terms with OpenAI rather than relying on assumed entitlements.
  • Consider what people may enter into chats. Review OpenAI's current data controls and your organization's own privacy, security, and retention requirements before sharing confidential or personal information.

What to evaluate before adopting

  • Test representative tasks with the context and source material your team expects to use.
  • Define which outputs need human review, citation checking, or approval before they are shared or used for decisions.
  • Check account administration, data controls, current plan features, usage limits, and total costs for the intended team.
  • If building a customer-facing assistant, separately scope the API integration, source-of-truth content, privacy controls, escalation path, testing, and ongoing maintenance.
