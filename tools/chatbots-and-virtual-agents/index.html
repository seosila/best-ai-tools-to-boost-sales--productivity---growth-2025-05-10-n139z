Chatbots and Virtual Agents

This category currently lists ChatGPT, a general-purpose AI assistant. That is a different buying choice from deploying a customer-facing support agent: start with the task, the audience, and the amount of workflow control you need.

Choose by the job you need done

General-purpose assistance

ChatGPT may suit individuals or teams who want a flexible assistant for drafting, summarizing, explaining concepts, brainstorming, and other knowledge-work tasks. It is useful when a person can guide the conversation, review the answer, and decide what to do next.

Customer-facing support

For answering visitors or customers inside a support workflow, evaluate products designed for that operation: Intercom Fin, Gorgias, or Tidio. Compare their knowledge setup, escalation, channel coverage, ticket workflows, and usage costs rather than assuming a general assistant includes them.

What to evaluate before adopting

  • Task and audience: Decide whether people will use the assistant directly or whether it must respond to your own customers as part of a service workflow.
  • Human review: Set expectations for checking factual, sensitive, or customer-facing answers. AI output can be wrong or misleading, and fluent wording does not establish accuracy.
  • Data and governance: Review what information users may enter, applicable data controls, workspace administration, retention, and your organization's privacy and security requirements before sharing business or personal data.
  • Product scope and access: Confirm the capabilities, usage limits, and controls available to your users and plan directly with OpenAI. Do not assume every capability is available in every account or configuration.
  • Integration needs: If you need an embedded customer chatbot, a connected support knowledge base, ticket actions, or managed handoff, check for those specific workflows. Building a custom application with OpenAI's API is separate from using the ChatGPT product.
