Instapage

Campaign landing pages, generation, and experimentation

Instapage

Instapage focuses on creating landing pages for marketing campaigns, with a builder, page templates, and tools for experimentation. Its Prompt to Page generator can create a page draft from a description of the offer, audience, and goal, including layout and generated copy, colors, fonts, and images.

Explore Instapage features View plans and pricing

AI page generation is still rolling out

Instapage's official help article describes Prompt to Page as a beta feature that is being released gradually. Not every account has access; accounts without the “Create with AI” option can request early access. Generation allowances are tied to the subscription and reset on the billing cycle, so verify both account access and your remaining allowance before relying on it for production work.

Generated pages can be previewed and then edited in the regular builder. As with other prompt-based tools, the result is a draft: review claims, imagery, brand details, mobile layouts, forms, and accessibility before publishing.

Experiments and plan boundaries

Instapage calls its A/B testing workflow “Experiments.” Its help documentation says A/B testing and AI Experiments are available only with some subscriptions, and an upgrade prompt appears when an account does not have access. The Convert plan's listed exclusive benefits include AI Experiments, personalization, and heatmaps. Check the current plans page or your subscription before assuming a particular testing or heatmap feature is included; exact entitlements and limits are not universal.

Who should consider Instapage?

Instapage may fit campaign teams that need dedicated landing-page production and want to combine a visual builder with measured experiments. It is also worth evaluating if an AI-generated first draft is useful and your account has access to the beta. It may be a poor fit if you require guaranteed immediate access to Prompt to Page, or if the subscription available to you does not include the experiments, traffic capacity, or team features you need. Compare Unbounce for Smart Traffic routing and Leadpages for AI-assisted site as well as landing-page creation.

Product and plan details checked 14 October 2026. Prompt to Page remains a phased beta; access, generation allowances, experiment access, and subscription details may change.
