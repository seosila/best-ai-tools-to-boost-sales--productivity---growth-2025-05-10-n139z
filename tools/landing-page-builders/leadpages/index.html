Leadpages

Leadpages combines landing-page publishing with website creation. Its AI Builder can generate a page, a new website, or a page added to an existing site from a prompt, then open the result for editing. The product therefore spans focused campaign pages and broader site-building workflows.

AI generation and editing

The AI Builder creates page layout, copy, images, and styling from a natural-language prompt. Users can select a standalone landing page, create a new website, or add a page to an existing site; generated pages can then be revised in the editor, including through AI chat. A Brand Kit and connected integrations can provide additional context when available.

Leadpages' pricing page lists the AI editor across its publishing plans, while each generation uses credits from a monthly allowance. Check current credit availability and any top-up terms in your account and on the pricing page.

Publishing versus optimization plans

The vendor separates publishing and optimization features. Pro and Business are publishing plans; manual A/B testing, dynamic text, personalization, anonymous-traffic detection, and lead enrichment start on Grow. Optimize adds Smart Traffic (AI-based variant routing), click and scroll heatmaps, auto-personalization, and multivariate testing. These are different capabilities: AI page generation creates a draft, manual A/B testing compares variants, and Smart Traffic routes visitors among variants.

The boundaries and limits are plan-specific and can change. Review the current pricing comparison for page, domain, usage, integration, and testing entitlements rather than assuming every feature is included with AI page creation.

Who should consider Leadpages?

Leadpages may suit small businesses and marketers who want to publish focused campaign pages or a basic multi-page site and prefer AI-assisted drafting. It is especially relevant when publishing needs may later expand to testing or traffic optimization, provided the higher plan fits the budget and feature needs. Teams needing only standalone landing pages should compare the available website features against their requirements; teams that need Smart Traffic or heatmaps should account for the Optimize tier. Compare Unbounce for campaign pages with Smart Traffic, and Instapage for its phased-beta Prompt to Page workflow.
