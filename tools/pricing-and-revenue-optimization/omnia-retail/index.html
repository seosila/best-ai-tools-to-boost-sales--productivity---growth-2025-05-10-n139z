Omnia Retail

Retail pricing strategy and automation

Omnia Retail

Omnia Retail provides pricing software for retailers that need to monitor markets and manage price strategies across products and channels. Its product materials describe competitor and market data, configurable pricing rules, automation, and insights that help pricing teams understand market positions and rule-driven changes.

Visit Omnia Retail Contact Omnia about pricing

Rules, market inputs, and pricing operations

Omnia describes pricing strategies built from configurable rules and market information, with automation executing within retailer-defined logic. The product's Pricing Strategy Tree is presented as a way to build and refine rules, while current product materials describe monitoring competitor moves, margin risks, and products that require attention. This can suit teams coordinating pricing and category decisions across a substantial assortment or multiple channels.

Omnia Retail's public material also distinguishes recommendations and insights from automatic price changes: teams establish and approve strategies, and automation acts within those defined rules. Validate the data inputs, rule precedence, margin floors, exception handling, and review process for your own operation before enabling changes.

Fit and implementation considerations

The relevant scope depends on assortment size, markets, channels, integrations, competitor coverage, and the complexity of pricing policies. A small seller needing only occasional manual competitor checks may find a retail pricing automation platform broader than necessary. Enterprise and multichannel buyers should clarify data onboarding, integration scope, services, contractual limits, and ongoing ownership of pricing rules.

The current official pricing page provides a contact-oriented pricing path; the stated SMB starting price of €399/month could not be independently confirmed from the page content available during this review, so it is not quoted here. Request a current written proposal and confirm currency, VAT treatment, scope, and product entitlements directly with Omnia.
