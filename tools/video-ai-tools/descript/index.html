Transcript-based video and podcast editing

Descript

Descript is a video and audio editor built around transcripts. It transcribes recordings so users can edit spoken content by editing text, and adds AI-assisted tools for tasks such as removing filler words, improving audio, creating clips, generating scripts, and refining video. It is most relevant to teams that publish interviews, podcasts, tutorials, demos, webinars, or talking-head content.

What Descript does

The transcript is the main editing surface: removing or moving words in the text changes the corresponding audio or video. AI tools can help with sound cleanup, filler-word removal, clips, scripts, captions, and other production steps. Availability and usage allowances vary by plan, so verify the exact features and AI-credit limits you need.

Who may find it useful

Descript is worth evaluating for content marketers, podcasters, educators, agencies, and small teams that regularly turn recorded speech into polished video or short-form clips. It may not be the first choice when the primary goal is generating cinematic scenes from prompts or making complex visual changes to footage.

Plans and billing

Descript's plans use per-person pricing and include limits such as media hours and AI credits, with different export and feature allowances. The pricing page presents monthly and annual billing options; compare the actual billing period, included hours, resolution, team seats, and top-up costs rather than relying on a single headline price.

Strengths and points to check

  • Transcript-based editing can speed up editing when the content is primarily spoken dialogue.
  • Useful AI helpers do not remove the need to review transcript accuracy, names, captions, voice edits, and final timing.
  • Check media-hour allowances, AI-credit usage, export resolution, collaboration, and translation or dubbing access on your plan.
  • Review consent and rights before using voice cloning, synthetic speech, or footage containing customers and employees.
