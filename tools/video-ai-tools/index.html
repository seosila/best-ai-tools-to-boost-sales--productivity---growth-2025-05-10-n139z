Video & Multimedia Marketing AI Tools

Video tools with AI can serve very different jobs. Some generate new footage from prompts or images; others help creators edit recordings, remove filler words, improve audio, and turn long videos into shorter clips. Start with the workflow you need rather than assuming every AI video product is a full video editor.

Choose by production workflow

Evaluate whether you need net-new generated scenes, edits to existing footage, transcript-based editing, social clips, subtitles, voice tools, or collaboration. Also compare generation credits, export resolution, media-hour limits, watermark rules, commercial-use terms, and how much manual review the output needs.
