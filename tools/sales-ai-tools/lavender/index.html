AI sales email coaching and personalization

Lavender

Lavender is an AI email coach for sales teams. Rather than running the outreach campaign, it helps a seller improve an individual sales email with writing feedback, personalization assistance, and analytics.

Last verified: 14 October 2026

Visit official website View official pricing

Email coaching for individual reps and teams

Lavender's official plan information describes feedback and coaching for sales emails, a personalization assistant, mobile editing, and individual analytics. Its team offering adds team insights and a leaderboard for coaching and enablement. The goal is to guide the person writing the message, not to replace campaign execution or guarantee a particular reply rate.

Supported email and sales platforms

Lavender lists Gmail and Outlook.com integrations for its individual Starter plan. Its Pro plan lists broader integrations including Outreach, HubSpot, Salesloft, Apollo, and Groove. Check the current plan page for the exact integration and feature availability that applies to your account.

Pricing model

The official pricing page currently lists a free Basic plan with five email analyses and five uses of the personalization assistant per month. Starter is listed at $29 monthly or $319 annually, and Pro at $49 monthly or $539 annually. Lavender Teams is listed as starting at $8,500; confirm the term, seat scope, and current offer with the vendor. Prices and plan details can change.

Strengths and points to check

  • It focuses on improving email drafts and coaching reps, which is distinct from software built to sequence and send campaigns.
  • Starter is limited to Gmail and Outlook.com; teams needing other listed sales platforms should compare the Pro plan.
  • The free plan has monthly usage limits. Review plan allowances and test whether its feedback fits your team's writing workflow.

Who should consider this tool?

Consider Lavender if sellers already write one-to-one or outbound sales emails and want help reviewing clarity, personalization, and message structure. Teams primarily needing mailbox rotation, automated sequences, or reply management should compare campaign platforms such as Smartlead or Instantly instead.

Features, availability, and pricing may change. Confirm current details directly with Lavender before making a decision.
