Rebuy

Rebuy is a Shopify-focused commerce platform for tailoring product discovery and shopping experiences. Merchants can evaluate it for product recommendations, bundles, cart experiences, and offers across the storefront and post-purchase journey.

Visit Rebuy View official pricing

Personalized storefront and cart experiences

Rebuy's product materials describe personalized product recommendations, dynamic bundles, promotions, a customizable Smart Cart with upsell options, checkout and post-purchase offers, search and collection experiences, and tools for flows and A/B testing. Which products and functions are available depends on the package and store setup.

Platform and workflow fit

Rebuy positions its merchandising platform for Shopify stores. Its focus is what shoppers see and can add during the buying journey; it is not an email-marketing platform. A merchant comparing retention tools should separately evaluate customer messaging products such as Klaviyo. Check Rebuy's current documentation for package requirements and the integrations needed by your storefront.

Pricing and trial

Rebuy's pricing page currently says packages start at $25 per month and that each package is priced according to monthly Shopify orders. Merchants can select among packages for cart and merchandising, checkout and post-purchase, search and collections, and flows and A/B testing; bundling packages may change the total. The page offers a 14-day trial for packages. Treat the starting figure as an entry point rather than a universal price and confirm the current package, usage terms, and total for your store on Rebuy's official pricing page.

Strengths and trade-offs

  • It brings several onsite merchandising and personalization jobs together, which may suit stores that want coordinated recommendations, cart offers, and post-purchase options.
  • Package selection and order-based pricing mean merchants should map the features they need and their monthly order volume before estimating cost.
  • Personalization is only as useful as the products, rules, and storefront placements a merchant configures; vendor revenue claims are not a guarantee of results for a particular store.
  • It is not a substitute for customer email or mobile-message campaigns.

Who should consider Rebuy?

Shopify merchants looking to add or coordinate product recommendations, bundles, cart upsells, or post-purchase offers may want to compare Rebuy's packages with their catalog, theme, and order volume. A store that only needs routine admin help could start with Shopify Sidekick; a store seeking competitor price intelligence should evaluate a dedicated option such as Prisync instead.

Features, package composition, trial terms, and pricing may change. Confirm current requirements and pricing directly with Rebuy before making a decision.
