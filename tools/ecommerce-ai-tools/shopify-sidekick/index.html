Shopify Sidekick

Shopify store assistant

Shopify Sidekick

Shopify Sidekick is a conversational assistant inside Shopify admin. Merchants can ask questions about their store, get guidance, work with analytics, create content, and complete supported admin tasks using everyday language.

Visit Shopify Sidekick View Shopify plans

What Sidekick can help with

Shopify's documentation describes Sidekick for store guidance and tasks such as analyzing store data, generating reports, editing products, managing supported order details, creating content, and building custom apps. It works in the context of the merchant's Shopify admin and presents changes for review before they are applied. The specific actions available depend on the task and store.

Sidekick and Shopify Magic

Sidekick is Shopify's conversational assistant, while Shopify Magic refers to Shopify's broader set of AI-powered features. They are related parts of Shopify's product experience, not separate vendors or standalone subscriptions to compare as independent tools.

Access and pricing

Shopify describes Sidekick as included with Shopify plans rather than sold as a separate subscription. Access to particular features and usage can vary by plan, store, and feature availability, so review Shopify's current plan and Sidekick documentation before relying on a specific capability. Shopify plan charges are separate from any other apps or services a store uses.

Strengths and limitations

  • It is available in the context of Shopify admin, which can make it relevant for merchants who already run their store there.
  • It supports a range of admin, analytics, and content tasks rather than specializing in a single marketing channel.
  • It is not an independent ecommerce platform, and supported tasks and access are subject to Shopify's current feature availability.
  • Merchants should review proposed changes and verify generated content or analysis before using it with customers or making business decisions.

Who should consider Shopify Sidekick?

Shopify merchants who want conversational help with routine store operations, reporting, or content may find Sidekick relevant, from small shops to larger teams already using Shopify. It may not be the right fit if you need a platform-independent assistant, a dedicated email-marketing system, onsite recommendation merchandising, or competitor price monitoring.

Features, access, and Shopify plan details can change. Confirm current availability with Shopify before making a decision.
