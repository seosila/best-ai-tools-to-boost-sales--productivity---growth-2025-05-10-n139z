Klaviyo

Klaviyo provides customer data and marketing tools for businesses, including ecommerce teams. Its platform supports customer profiles and marketing communication workflows such as email and mobile messaging.

What Klaviyo does

Klaviyo describes a platform for customer data and marketing. Businesses can evaluate it for managing customer profiles and coordinating email and mobile-message marketing. Available channels, features, and usage depend on the selected plan and current product terms.

Who may find it useful

Ecommerce businesses considering customer-data-led marketing may want to compare Klaviyo's supported channels, profile limits, message allowances, integrations, and billing against their own audience and campaign needs.

Free plan and pricing

Klaviyo's pricing page lists a free plan for up to 250 active profiles and 500 email sends per month. It also describes limited allowances for mobile messaging and Composer use. Paid pricing and included usage vary with active profiles, channel usage, plan, billing terms, and region; confirm the live pricing page for current details.

Klaviyo's partner program serves its partner ecosystem. Its existence alone does not establish a general affiliate commission, so no affiliate commission is claimed here.

Points to check before choosing Klaviyo

  • Estimate active profiles and monthly email volume, then compare them with current plan limits.
  • Check mobile-message allowances, usage pricing, and regional terms for each channel you plan to use.
  • Review the official partner information separately from pricing; it is not a guarantee of affiliate compensation.
Features, limits, and pricing may change. Confirm current details directly with Klaviyo before making a decision.
