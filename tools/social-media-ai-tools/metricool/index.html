Social publishing, analytics, and reporting

Metricool

Metricool combines social media planning and publishing with account analytics, competitor research, and reporting. It is designed for marketers who need to manage social channels and understand performance in one place. Its AI features support content workflows, and Metricool announced official connectors for ChatGPT and Claude in September 2026, allowing users to work with connected account data and content planning through conversational interfaces.

Visit MetricoolView pricing

What Metricool helps you do

Metricool's feature set includes a content calendar, scheduled publishing, analytics, competitor profile analysis, and downloadable reports, with capabilities varying by network and plan. Its official pricing page also lists an AI social media assistant on the free plan. The September 2026 connector announcement describes using ChatGPT or Claude to review account performance, plan content, and work with scheduling information after connecting an account.

Pricing and plan limits

Metricool's current public pricing page advertises a free plan for one brand, up to twenty scheduled posts per month, analysis of five competitor profiles, thirty days of analytics, and an AI social media assistant. Paid plans expand brand limits and add capabilities such as more competitor analysis, historical analytics, team/client access, and reporting. Confirm current network exclusions, add-ons, quotas, and billing terms on the official pricing page before choosing a plan.

Strengths and considerations

  • A strong candidate when publishing needs to be evaluated alongside analytics, competitor tracking, and reports.
  • Network support, historical data, and account limits differ by plan, so verify the details for each social channel you use.
  • Its AI and conversational connectors can help surface account insights, but business decisions still require checking the underlying data and context.
  • Compare Buffer for a simpler channel-based publishing workflow, or Predis.ai when AI-generated creative assets are the primary need.

Who should consider Metricool?

Freelancers, social media managers, small businesses, and agencies that need a combination of scheduling, analytics, competitor monitoring, and client reporting should consider Metricool. It may be more than necessary for a creator who only needs a lightweight posting queue.
