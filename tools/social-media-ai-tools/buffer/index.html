Buffer

Social publishing and AI-assisted writing

Buffer

Buffer is a social media publishing workspace for creators, small businesses, and teams that want to plan, write, schedule, and manage posts across supported channels. Its AI Assistant helps brainstorm ideas, rewrite drafts, and tailor copy, while the publishing calendar and queue help keep a consistent schedule. Buffer is primarily a publishing and content workflow tool rather than a deep social-listening platform.

Visit BufferView pricing

What Buffer helps you do

Buffer supports scheduled publishing to networks including Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Threads, YouTube, Pinterest, X, and other supported channels. The workflow combines post ideas and drafts with a calendar, queue, and collaboration features. Check the supported channel and format for each account before building a campaign around it.

Pricing and plan limits

Buffer's public pricing page currently advertises a free plan for up to three channels, with up to ten scheduled posts per channel and access to its AI Assistant. Paid plans are priced by channel and add capabilities such as unlimited scheduled posts, advanced analytics, and team collaboration depending on the plan. Verify current plan limits, billing terms, and channel availability on Buffer's official pricing page.

Strengths and considerations

  • A relatively straightforward fit for creators and small teams that prioritize planning and publishing over a large all-in-one marketing suite.
  • The AI Assistant can help draft and refine content, but teams should still review brand voice, claims, links, and platform-specific formatting.
  • Channel-based pricing can be economical for small setups, but costs can rise as the number of connected channels grows.
  • Compare Metricool if analytics, competitor tracking, and reporting are central to the workflow; compare Predis.ai if generating social-ready visuals and short videos is the main need.

Who should consider Buffer?

Creators, consultants, small businesses, and lean marketing teams that need a manageable publishing calendar and AI help with post drafts may find Buffer a useful starting point. Agencies should compare its team and approval features against the number of brands and channels they manage.
