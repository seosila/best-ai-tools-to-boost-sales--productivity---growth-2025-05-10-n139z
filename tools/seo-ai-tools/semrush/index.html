Semrush

SEO research and search visibility

Semrush

Semrush is a broad search and marketing platform for teams that want to research keywords and competitors, audit websites, and monitor organic rankings. Its current SEO and AI Search offering also includes AI-search visibility workflows, which should be evaluated separately from traditional SEO research and rank tracking.

Visit Semrush View SEO and AI Search plans

Research, audits, and ranking workflows

Traditional SEO workflows include keyword research and competitor analysis to find and compare search opportunities, site audits to identify technical or on-page issues, and position tracking to monitor rankings across selected targets. This breadth can help an in-house team or agency combine discovery and ongoing monitoring, but it also means teams should decide which modules and reporting workflows they will actually use.

AI-search visibility is a related but distinct area: it monitors brand presence and performance in supported AI search experiences and prompt-based views rather than simply reporting conventional search-engine positions. Semrush product pages describe this alongside SEO functionality in current SEO + AI Search / Semrush One packaging. The precise product names, eligible features, tracked prompts, report volumes, and project limits depend on the selected plan and may change.

Who should consider Semrush?

Semrush may fit marketers, SEO specialists, and agencies that need keyword research, competitor context, technical auditing, and rank monitoring in one vendor environment. Teams focused mainly on content optimization may also compare Surfer SEO; teams needing detailed crawl-level diagnosis should evaluate Screaming Frog SEO Spider alongside it.

Its breadth may be unnecessary if the immediate task is a single technical crawl or a focused content brief. Before purchase, map your required projects, users, keyword volumes, AI visibility reporting, historical data, and exports to the live plan comparison. Do not assume traditional SEO access includes every AI visibility feature or usage allowance.
