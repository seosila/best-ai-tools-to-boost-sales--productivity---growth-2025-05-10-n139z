AI Video Tools for Marketing: Runway vs Descript

Runway and Descript both use AI in video production, but they solve different problems. Runway is a generation-first option for creating or transforming visual footage. Descript is an editing-first option for recordings where speech, transcripts, audio cleanup, and short clips matter. Choose according to the work your team needs to do repeatedly.

When Runway is the better fit

Consider Runway when your brief starts with a scene, image, or visual effect rather than an existing transcript. It supports video generation from prompts and images and offers tools to extend or modify footage. Before committing to a plan, estimate the number of generations and revisions required; the credits consumed by a short clip can vary by model and settings.

When Descript is the better fit

Consider Descript when most of the value is in what people say: editing an interview by changing its transcript, cleaning audio, removing filler words, captioning, or producing shorter clips from long recordings. Check the plan's media hours, AI-credit limits, export resolution, collaboration features, and any additional usage costs.

A practical test before buying

  1. Pick one representative task you repeat each week: for example, a 20-second product scene or a 10-minute customer interview.
  2. Run the task on the plan you would actually buy, and record the time from first input to export—not just generation time.
  3. Count revisions, failed attempts, credit or media-hour usage, and manual correction time.
  4. Check watermark and export restrictions, commercial-use terms, privacy handling, and whether the output fits your ad or social platform's format.
  5. Compare the cost per usable final asset rather than the monthly subscription alone.

Important limits

Neither tool guarantees accurate brand details or a publish-ready result. Review generated products, faces, text, captions, and audio before publication. Pricing, models, and feature limits change; use the official pricing pages for current terms. This is a workflow comparison, not a hands-on benchmark or a claim that one tool is universally better.
